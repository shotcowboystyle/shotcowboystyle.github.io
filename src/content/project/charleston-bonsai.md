Charleston Bonsai Co. sells finished trees, one specimen at a time, and the site is built like a catalog of individual pieces rather than a grid of products. Each tree carries its own facts (age, height, class) beside its photograph, set at the same weight as the botanical name.

I built and run it end to end: architecture, delivery, hosting and ongoing production support, on Nuxt, Vue and TypeScript with Supabase behind it.
