Clean Break is an iPhone app for after a breakup. It finds the places an ex still surfaces (Photos Memories, shared albums, social feeds) and walks through hiding or removing each one at a pace the person sets. Nothing is permanent, and the ex is never notified.

The design is shaped by what iOS actually allows. Hidden photos cannot be read back, undo only lives as long as the session, and no app can draw over another, so every walkthrough is complete before the person leaves for Settings or Facebook.
